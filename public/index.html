UNDER THE SURFACE

The full story, faithfully restored.

Chronicle replays VS Code's persisted session log and Copilot Agent event streams, joining available transcripts, debug events, model metadata, and editing checkpoints.

Reasoning is shown only when its text was actually saved. Opaque reasoning IDs cannot be decoded. Compacted, missing, or truncated source data cannot be recovered; raw records and source warnings remain available.
